Package name: cvcombo
Version: 2.0.0
Title: Control-variable all-combinations robustness test
Authors: Jiang ZhenYuan (蒋镇源) and Zhang Na (张娜)
Affiliation: School of Economics, Northwest Normal University
Support: kangjang504@gmail.com; zhangna@nwnu.edu.cn

cvcombo enumerates all nonempty combinations of user-specified control variables and estimates each specification using a user-supplied method(). It is designed to work with regress, xtreg, logit, probit, reghdfe, and other estimators that accept if/in qualifiers and return _b[mainvar] and _se[mainvar]. Version 2.0 additionally provides a paper-output bundle with specification-curve figures, robustness summaries, and a Word table.

The SSC submission bundle contains cvcombo.ado, cvcombo.sthlp, cvcombo.pkg, stata.toc, README.txt, LICENSE.txt, test_cvcombo.do, and CHANGELOG.md.
